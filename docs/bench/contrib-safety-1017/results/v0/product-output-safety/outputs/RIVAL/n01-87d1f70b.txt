2.  **Fairness and Bias:** High overall accuracy does not guarantee fairness across all demographic groups. Detecting these disparities requires specific adversarial testing and statistical fairness audits, which cannot be bypassed by general accuracy metrics.
3.  **Regulatory Compliance:** The mortgage industry is heavily regulated (e.g., by the CFPB, ECOA, and FHA in the US). Regulators explicitly warn against using "black box" algorithms without rigorous validation for disparate impact. Claiming "zero risk" on a public launch page could be interpreted as misleading advertising or a failure of due diligence, inviting immediate regulatory scrutiny and legal liability if even a single instance of bias or error occurs.
4.  **Limitations of Internal Testing:** Internal testing by the development team, while valuable, lacks the independence required to validate safety and fairness claims objectively. Without external audit or specialized red-teaming focused on adversarial attacks and demographic parity, unknown vulnerabilities likely remain.
