# Bias Review and Mitigation Plan for Interview Coach

**1. Data Audit and Gap Analysis (Week 1)**
*   **Demographic Mapping:** Since you are using a fixed dataset, immediately catalog the demographic attributes of every speaker (gender, race/ethnicity, age, native language, accent, and disability status if known). If these labels are missing, you cannot measure bias; you must pause integration until the dataset is annotated by a diverse human panel.
*   **Representation Check:** Calculate the distribution of these groups. If any group represents less than 15% of the data, the model will likely underperform for them. Identify these gaps immediately.
*   **Speech Pattern Inventory:** Tag samples for specific linguistic features often penalized by AI, such as code-switching, non-standard grammar, pauses for thought, or varying speech rates common in different cultures.

**2. Baseline Model Evaluation (Weeks 2–3)**
*   **Disaggregated Scoring:** Run your current algorithm on the dataset but do not look only at the average accuracy. Break down the scores by each demographic group identified in step 1.
*   **Statistical Parity Check:** Compare the average "confidence" and "communication" scores across groups. If candidates with Accent A consistently score 10% lower than those with Accent B despite similar human-rated quality, you have a confirmed bias.
*   **False Positive/Negative Analysis:** Determine if the model systematically flags nervousness in one group as "low confidence" while interpreting similar behaviors in another group as "thoughtful pausing."

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Validation (Weeks 3–4)**
*   **Blind Review Panel:** Assemble a review panel reflecting the diversity of your target user base. Have them score a random subset of 50–100 videos without seeing the AI's score.
*   **Correlation Study:** Compare human scores against AI scores per demographic group. A low correlation for a specific group indicates the model is misinterpreting their communication style.
*   **Qualitative Feedback:** Ask the panel to identify specific moments where the AI's likely logic (e.g., penalizing lack of eye contact or specific hand gestures) conflicts with cultural norms of respect or engagement.

**4. Mitigation Strategies (Weeks 4–5)**
*   **Data Augmentation:** If specific groups are underrepresented or consistently mis-scored, prioritize acquiring or synthesizing more practice data for those specific profiles to retrain or fine-tune the model.
*   **Feature Suppression:** If the model relies heavily on acoustic features correlated with accent rather than semantic content, work with your engineers to reduce the weight of those specific features in the scoring formula.
*   **Calibration Layers:** Apply post-processing calibration. If Group X is systematically scored 5 points lower, apply a statistical correction factor to equalize the distribution, ensuring the score reflects relative skill within the group rather than absolute alignment with a dominant norm.
*   **Confidence Intervals:** Instead of a single precise number, display scores as a range (e.g., "75–85") with a disclaimer that the tool is a practice aid, not a definitive judgment, to reduce psychological harm from outliers.

**5. Pre-Release Safeguards (Week 6)**
*   **Hard Limits:** Implement logic that prevents the system from generating extremely low scores for users who speak clearly but have non-standard patterns, flagging these for human review if possible, or capping the penalty.
*   **User Feedback Loop:** Build a "Disagree with Score" button in the beta. Allow users to explain why they feel the score was unfair. This data is critical for post-launch iteration.
*   **Transparency Documentation:** Publish a "Model Card" for users explaining what the tool measures, what it does not measure (e.g., cultural nuance), and the known limitations regarding accents and dialects.

**Note:** Given the six-week deadline, if significant bias is found in Week 3 that cannot be mitigated by calibration, you must delay the launch or disable the automated scoring feature for affected demographics to prevent reputational and ethical harm.
Do not release the tool with a "black box" scorer; unknown bias is a greater risk than no scorer at all.
