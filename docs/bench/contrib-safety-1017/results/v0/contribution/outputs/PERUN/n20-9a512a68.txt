Subject: Clarification on recurring payment timing and grace period behavior

Hi Ravi,

Thank you so much for opening this issue and for taking the time to investigate the recurring payment system. We truly appreciate new contributors like you helping us stress-test the platform. It is completely understandable why the behavior you observed looked like a bug; on the surface, a payment that does not process on the exact due date can certainly appear to be "skipped."

After reviewing your report against the project's documented architecture, I wanted to offer some clarification that might resolve the confusion. The OpenSource Billing project includes a designed **grace period** feature. This is an intentional safety mechanism that delays payment processing slightly after the due date to accommodate bank transfer variances and prevent premature failure notifications for users who are merely a few hours late.

What you identified as a "skipped" payment is actually the system correctly engaging this grace period. The payment is not lost or ignored; it is held in a pending state until the grace window closes, at which point the standard processing logic takes over. This behavior is described in the `docs/payment-lifecycle.md` file under the "Grace Periods and Delays" section, though I admit this detail is easy to miss if you aren't looking specifically for it.

Since the system is functioning according to its design specifications, there isn't a code defect to fix here. However, your report highlights a very real usability issue: if a knowledgeable contributor like yourself mistook this feature for a bug, our documentation likely isn't explaining the grace period clearly enough.

Would you be open to us closing this specific issue as "working as intended," and perhaps opening a new, separate issue (or updating this one) to track a **documentation improvement**? We could use this as an opportunity to make the grace period behavior more prominent in the guides so future users and contributors aren't puzzled by the delay.

Thanks again for your sharp eye and for caring about the reliability of the billing system. We'd love to see you stick around, and clarifying these docs would be a fantastic first contribution!
