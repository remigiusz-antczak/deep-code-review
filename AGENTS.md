bar, the definition of done, and the hard confidentiality + no-fabrication rules
— live in **[`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)**, the single source of truth for working

divergent set. Start there, then see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
